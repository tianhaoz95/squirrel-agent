Cross-State Licensing & Reciprocity

If you live in one state but purchase investment properties in another, managing your licenses across state lines is key. Here is how reciprocity works, with a focus on California (CA) and Texas (TX):

The CA-TX Border (No Reciprocity)

Neither California nor Texas offers reciprocity. To practice in both, you must apply as a new applicant in each state. However, there is one key exam exemption:

  • California Agent getting licensed in Texas: Texas requires you to complete the 180 hours of education, but allows you to waive the National portion of the Texas licensing exam. You only take the Texas State exam.
  • Texas Agent getting licensed in California: California does not grant any waivers. You must complete the 135 hours of education and pass the entire 150-question California exam.
  • Residency: Neither state requires local residency. You can apply as a non-resident by submitting an Out-of-State Consent to Service of Process form.

National Reciprocity Categories

Full Reciprocity

Transfer easily with a state-law exam. (e.g., Colorado, Virginia, Alabama, Mississippi)

Partial Reciprocity

Reciprocal agreements only exist between specific states. (e.g., Florida, Georgia, Illinois)

No Reciprocity

No recognition of out-of-state licenses. Full course and exams required. (e.g., California, Texas, New York)
